Once you know [where AI answers come from](/learn/how-ai-recommends-local-businesses/), the playbook writes itself: make your business the easiest one to verify and the clearest one to quote. Here's the checklist, in order of impact.

## 1. Become one consistent fact

Software stitching you together from many sources needs those sources to agree. One exact business name, one phone number, one set of hours — identical on your website, Google profile, directories and socials. Every mismatch is a seam where the stitching fails, and the recommendation goes to the tidier competitor.

## 2. Keep your Google profile and reviews thriving

Assistants lean heavily on the same signals as Maps: a complete, active profile and a steady flow of genuine reviews with replies. The [review routine](/learn/how-to-get-more-google-reviews/) pulls double duty here — every review is both social proof for humans and evidence for machines. Reviews that mention your suburb and the job type are especially quotable.

## 3. Say the obvious things in plain sentences

AI quotes text, so write the sentences you want quoted. "Fat Yak Plumbing serves Northside, Westgate and the inner suburbs, specialising in emergency repairs and hot-water systems" is machine-usable; a hero banner reading "Excellence. Delivered." is not. Every important fact about your business should exist somewhere as one clear, complete sentence.

## 4. Answer real questions directly

Assistants assemble answers, so pages that *are* answers get used. Take the questions customers ask you every week and give each a page that answers it in the first breath, then adds the detail. (You're reading exactly this pattern right now — it's how this page is built.) This is where a [question-answering content plan](/services/content/) earns its keep twice: rankings today, AI citations alongside.

## 5. Label your pages for machines

Structured data is a behind-the-scenes label that tells software, unambiguously: this is a business, here's its name, area, services, questions and answers. Invisible to visitors, load-bearing for machines. It's built into [every site we ship](/services/web-design/) — retrofit it if your site predates it.

## 6. Be mentioned where AI reads

Local news, industry directories, supplier pages, community sites — third-party mentions are the corroboration that turns your claims into facts. A handful of genuine local mentions outweighs a hundred junk listings.
