The second question every business owner asks (right after "what does it cost") is "how long will it take". Here are real numbers, and what they depend on.

## The quick wins: days to weeks

Fixing your Google Business Profile — right category, complete details, photos, first reviews — often moves Maps visibility within weeks, because Google processes profile changes quickly. Technical repairs to a website (unblocking pages, fixing speed) also register fast once Google revisits. If your problem was one of these, you'll feel it in the first month.

## The real build: two to three months

New pages — covering your services and each area you serve — typically take 60 to 90 days to find their level. Google needs to discover the page, test it against searchers, and watch the results. During this stretch it can feel like nothing is happening. Something is: evidence is accumulating.

## The compounding phase: six to twelve months

Competitive searches — "electrician + your city" against established rivals — are won over seasons, not sprints. Each month stacks on the last: more pages answering more questions, more reviews, more mentions. This is also where the economics flip. Ads cost the same every month forever; rankings, once earned, keep sending customers with the meter off.

## What changes the timeline

Three things, mostly. **Your starting point** — an established site with history moves faster than a fresh domain. **Your competition** — a country town is a different race from an inner-city market. **Your patience for the boring work** — the businesses that show up in month nine are the ones that didn't stop in month three.
